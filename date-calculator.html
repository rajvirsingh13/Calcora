How to Use the Date Calculator

Calcora's Date Calculator can be used to find the number of days between two dates.

  • Choose a start date.
  • Choose an end date.
  • Tap Calculate Difference.
  • View the total number of days and weeks.

You can also use the Add or Subtract Days section to find a date that occurs a certain number of days before or after another date.

How Does the Date Difference Work?

The calculator converts the selected calendar dates into a common time reference and calculates the difference between them.

Date Difference = End Date − Start Date

The result is displayed as the absolute number of calendar days between the two selected dates. If the second date is earlier, the calculator also indicates the direction of the difference.

The number of days between two dates can vary depending on the month and whether a leap year is involved.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days are between two dates?

Enter the two dates into the calculator and it will calculate their calendar-day difference automatically.

Does the calculator account for leap years?

Yes. The calculation is based on actual calendar dates, so leap days are included when applicable.

Can I find a date 30 days from another date?

Yes. Enter the starting date and the number of days, then choose Add Days.

Can I calculate a date in the past?

Yes. Enter the starting date and number of days, then choose Subtract Days.
