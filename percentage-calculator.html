Percentage Formulas

Percentage of a Number

Percentage Result = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number

What Percentage Is One Number of Another?

Percentage Increase or Decrease

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a percentage?

A percentage represents a number as a part of 100. The symbol used for percentage is %.

How do I calculate 10% of a number?

Divide 10 by 100 and multiply the result by the number. For example, 10% of 200 is 20.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the original value, and multiply by 100.
