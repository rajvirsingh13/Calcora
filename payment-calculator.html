  • Enter the loan term in years.
  • Press Calculate Payment.

Payment Calculator Formula

For a standard fixed-rate loan with monthly payments, the monthly payment can be calculated using the following formula:

M = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

Where:

  • M = Monthly payment
  • P = Principal loan amount
  • r = Monthly interest rate
  • n = Number of monthly payments

The annual interest rate is converted to a monthly rate before the calculation.

Payment Calculator vs EMI Calculator

A payment calculator and EMI calculator can use the same standard fixed-rate monthly payment formula. The terminology may vary depending on the type of loan and the lender.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a payment calculator calculate?

It estimates the monthly payment and can also show the total interest and total amount paid over the selected loan term.

Does a longer loan term reduce the monthly payment?

Generally, a longer loan term lowers the monthly payment, but it can result in more interest being paid over the full term.

Does the interest rate affect monthly payments?

Yes. For the same loan amount and term, a higher interest rate generally produces a higher monthly payment.

Can I use this calculator for different types of loans?

Yes, it can be used to estimate payments for many standard fixed-interest loans. Always check the lender's terms for the actual payment amount.
