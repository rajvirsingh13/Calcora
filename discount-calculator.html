How to Use the Discount Calculator

Calcora's Discount Calculator makes it easy to find out how much you will save during a sale or discount offer.

  • Enter the original price of the item.
  • Enter the discount percentage.
  • Tap the Calculate Discount button.
  • See the discount amount and final price.

How Is Discount Calculated?

The discount amount is calculated by multiplying the original price by the discount percentage.

Discount Amount = Original Price × (Discount % ÷ 100)

The final price is then calculated by subtracting the discount amount from the original price.

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Discount Calculator Example

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a 20% discount?

Multiply the original price by 20 and divide by 100 to find the discount amount. Subtract that amount from the original price to get the final price.

What is the formula for discount?

Discount amount = Original price × (Discount percentage ÷ 100).

How do I calculate the final price after a discount?

Subtract the discount amount from the original price.

Can I calculate a 100% discount?

Yes. A 100% discount results in a final price of zero.
