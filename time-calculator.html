How to Use the Time Calculator

Calcora's Time Calculator helps you quickly add or subtract two amounts of time.

  • Enter the hours, minutes and seconds for the first time.
  • Choose whether you want to add or subtract the second time.
  • Enter the hours, minutes and seconds for the second time.
  • Click Calculate Time to see the result.

How Time Calculation Works

Time is converted into seconds before the calculation. This makes addition and subtraction accurate even when minutes or seconds go above their normal ranges.

Total Seconds = Hours × 3,600 + Minutes × 60 + Seconds

After the calculation, the total seconds are converted back into hours, minutes and seconds so the result is easy to read.

Example

If you add 2 hours 30 minutes to 1 hour 45 minutes, the result is 4 hours 15 minutes.

Time Calculator FAQ

Can I add hours, minutes and seconds?

Yes. You can enter all three values and Calcora will calculate the combined time.

Can I subtract time?

Yes. Select "Subtract Time" and enter the second time. The calculator will subtract it from the first time.

What happens if the result is negative?

When subtracting a larger time from a smaller time, the result is shown as a negative duration while the total hours, minutes and seconds remain accurate.

Can I enter more than 59 minutes?

Yes. The calculator converts the complete value into seconds, so values such as 90 minutes are handled correctly.
