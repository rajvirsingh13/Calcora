How to Use the Loan Calculator

Enter three basic details to estimate the payment for a standard fixed-rate loan.

  • Enter the amount you want to borrow.
  • Enter the annual interest rate.
  • Enter the loan term in years.
  • Press Calculate Loan.

Calcora will calculate an estimated monthly payment along with the total interest and total amount paid.

Loan Payment Formula

For a standard fixed-rate loan with monthly payments, the estimated monthly payment is calculated using:

M = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

Where:

  • M = Monthly payment
  • P = Principal loan amount
  • r = Monthly interest rate
  • n = Total number of monthly payments

The annual interest rate is divided by 12 and then by 100 to obtain the monthly interest rate.

The final amount offered by a lender may be different because of processing fees, insurance, taxes, changing rates, or other loan conditions.

What Affects Your Loan Payment?

Loan Amount

Borrowing a larger amount generally results in a higher monthly payment when the interest rate and term remain unchanged.

Interest Rate

A higher interest rate generally increases both the monthly payment and total interest cost.

Loan Term

A longer repayment period generally lowers the monthly payment but can increase the total interest paid over the loan.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a loan calculator?

A loan calculator estimates the monthly payment and overall repayment cost of a loan based on the amount, interest rate, and repayment term.

Is the result an exact lender quote?

No. The result is an estimate based on the values entered. Actual lender payments can vary because of fees, taxes, insurance, rate changes, and loan-specific terms.

Does a longer loan term always save money?

Not necessarily. A longer term can reduce the monthly payment, but it may increase the total interest paid.

Can I use this calculator for a personal loan?

Yes. You can use it for many standard fixed-rate loans, including personal, car, home, and other installment loans.
