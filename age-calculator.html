How to Use the Age Calculator

Enter your date of birth and select Calculate Age. Calcora will calculate your age using today's date.

What Can You Find?

  • Your exact age in years, months and days.
  • Your total completed months.
  • Your total completed days.
  • The date of your next birthday.
  • The number of days remaining until your next birthday.

How Is Age Calculated?

Your age is calculated by comparing your date of birth with today's date. The calculator accounts for different month lengths and leap years when calculating the result.

The displayed age represents the number of completed years, months and days since your date of birth.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this Age Calculator?
The calculator uses the selected date of birth and your device's current date to calculate the result.
Can I calculate my age in days?
Yes. The calculator shows the total number of completed days since your date of birth.
Does the calculator account for leap years?
Yes. The date calculation takes leap years and different month lengths into account.
Can I use this calculator on my phone?
Yes. The page is designed to work on mobile phones, tablets and desktop screens.
